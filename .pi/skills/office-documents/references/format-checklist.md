# Format acceptance checklist

## DOCX

- Heading hierarchy and page breaks are intentional.
- Tables, lists, links, and images survive a save/reopen cycle.
- Headers, footers, and page numbers are preserved when editing a template.

## XLSX

- Formulas and sheet names are preserved unless the task changes them.
- Number formats, dates, frozen panes, and filters remain usable.
- Validate representative formulas and inspect the workbook after saving.
